Appendix A · Procedure

How to Play

Objective

One exhibit at a time. Decide: was it made by a person (REAL) or generated by a machine (AI)? Each correct call is worth 100 points. Each wrong call costs a life. You begin with three; the assessment ends when none remain.

Special Exhibits

Every tenth exhibit arrives in another medium, chosen at random: a song, a video, a passage of text, or a human voice. Judge it the same way. Listen or watch as many times as you need — the exhibit waits.

Controls

A or ←answer REAL
D or →answer AI
Enter or Nnext exhibit

Clicking the buttons also works, of course.

The Record

Your best score is kept on file. An assessment in progress survives a closed tab; only death resets it.
