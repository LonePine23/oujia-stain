A writing program

Deadline Gang is a writing program for writers who can't write but want to write. Right?

Over twelve weeks six playwrights meet fortnightly, with a deadline in place to produce a finished first draft.

It doesn't have to be perfect. It just has to be done.

This program focuses on fellowship-style support. Each meeting is chaired by a different member of the gang, and writers will be expected to update the group on their progress each session.
