Hue and Seek

Setup — five colours and a date per photograph. The sheet is the whole publishing step.

Add photographs to begin.

Each one gets five target colours, drawn from its own dominant regions and held apart so they read as five distinct hunts. Approve them, or click the photo to place one yourself.

This photograph

Setting a date drops this photograph onto that day and shifts everything from that day onward along by one — nothing else needs rejigging by hand. It pins itself there at the same time, so a later shuffle flows around it instead of moving it.

The five colours

Select a slot, then click anywhere on the photo to place that colour by hand. Arrow keys nudge one pixel. The photo dims everywhere the selected colour isn't, so you can see how much room a player will have.

Schedule

The filmstrip is the schedule — one photograph per day, running forward from the first date. Shuffle reorders and redates the lot, then pulls similar-looking photographs apart so you don't get four paintings in a row. Pinned dates stay put and everything else flows around them.

Export

Commit levels.csv next to index.html, and the photographs into images/. The game reads the sheet on load, so a new row is a new day with no rebuild.
