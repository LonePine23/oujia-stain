How to play

Inside the Owe & Behold First National Bank are the seven hardest safes in the world. Each holds $50,000 behind a single-word password. They get harder as you go.

Each safe

  • You get the password's definition and 30 seconds.
  • Type a word and press Enter. Guess as many times as you like until the clock runs out.
  • The closer your word is in meaning, the closer the dial stops to the click.
  • Forms of the password count: plurals, -ed and -ing, and other spellings.
  • Words that aren't in the codebook don't count.
  • Crack the password and the safe opens straight away. Otherwise, when the clock runs out, whatever is in the box is entered and you take what you've earned.

The take

Each safe pays out for your closest guess. Bad guesses never cost you anything.

The most you can take in a day is $350,000. Whatever you bank is yours to keep.

Gear

  • Blueprint shows how long the password is and its first letter.
  • Walkie Talkie gives you one word that's close on the dial.
  • Kaleidoscope gives you the password's letters, jumbled.
  • Each works once a day, on any safe. Choosing gear stops the clock while you decide. Using it resets the clock to 30 seconds.

Privacy

Visits to this page are counted with Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service that doesn't use cookies.

Your stats stay in this browser. When crew stats are switched on, the game sends only a random ID made in your browser, the date, your seven payouts (which show which safes you cracked), and which safe each piece of gear was used on. Results are deleted after 14 days. The crew's numbers are self-reported and can be faked. It's a heist, not a court of law.
