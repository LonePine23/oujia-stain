This export is one-way. Your calendar won't update by itself. If you add a plant, change a schedule or change settings, download a fresh file and replace the old events. Importing into a dedicated “Plants” calendar makes this easy: delete that calendar's events, then import the new file.

Settings

Winter multiplier

During a plant's rest season, its interval is multiplied by this and rounded to the nearest day. Most plants rest in winter; summer-resting plants (e.g. aeoniums) use the same multiplier during the opposite months. Set it to ×1.0 to turn this off.

Both months are included. The window can wrap past December (e.g. November to February). Summer is the same months six months later.
