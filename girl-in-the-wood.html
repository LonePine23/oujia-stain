Girl in the Wood

A spooky forest fairytale

Step into a darkly whimsical, edge-of-your-seat psychological thriller with Girl in the Wood, a spellbinding eco-horror drama that flips classic fairy-tale tropes completely on their head.

The story follows Peggy, a fiercely determined young girl who plunges into a perilous, enchanted European forest after her older brother, Chuck, is violently dragged into the shadows by a creature of pure evil known as Il Buio. Armed with nothing but her brother's knife and a sharp wit, Peggy forms a desperate alliance with Johnny Arkansas — a swaggering, directionally challenged cowboy hunting for his own long-lost father. Together, they must navigate a surreal gauntlet of eccentric and terrifying woodland horrors, from a bizarre, prophecy-spitting blind oracle to a pair of deceptive, ancient twin hunger demons.

To bargain for Chuck's soul, Peggy is forced to hunt down three mythical ingredients before a strict midnight deadline: the sharpest point, the oldest page, and her own heart. Written with a mischievous, fast-paced energy, the narrative effortlessly balances laugh-out-loud comedy — featuring absurd, knee-slapping duels with a band of animal-masked bandits — and suffocating, plastic-tendriled terror.

Enhanced by a haunting, live-scored musical atmosphere, Girl in the Wood is an imaginative, visually striking theatrical ride about the lengths we will go to save the people we love, proving that the deepest shadows can only be conquered by the very things born from them.
